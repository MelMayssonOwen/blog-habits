Patterns vs Streaks: which habit tracker should you pick?

Choose Streaks if you want a proven, one-time $5.99 purchase, deep Apple-ecosystem integration (Watch, Mac, Health), and you don't mind a streak that resets on a single missed day. Choose Patterns if you repeatedly quit trackers after one slip and want a streak that only breaks after two consecutive misses, plus if-then habit planning, accepting that it's a subscription ($49.99/yr or $9.99/mo). Both are Apple-only. Streaks is cheaper and simpler; Patterns is more forgiving by design.

Where Streaks wins

Price and reach. A one-time $5.99 (verified on the App Store) against a recurring $49.99/year is not a close call on cost, and over a few years the gap is large. Streaks also has a mature Apple Watch app and runs on Mac and Vision Pro, so you can tick a habit from your wrist. If you're disciplined enough that a hard streak reset motivates rather than defeats you, Streaks is the efficient choice.

Where Patterns wins

The failure mode it targets. The "never miss twice" rule (from James Clear) says one miss is fine, it's the second consecutive miss that starts a new habit of not doing it. Streaks' classic design resets to zero on the first miss, which for many people triggers the "well, I've ruined it" spiral. Patterns bakes the forgiveness in: the streak survives one off day. It also structures each habit as an implementation intention ("after I brush my teeth, I will…"), a format shown to raise follow-through. If a single missed day has ended every tracker you've tried, that design is worth more than the price gap.

Bottom line

Most people who already keep streaks should just buy Streaks, it's cheaper and excellent. Reach for Patterns specifically if perfectionism is what breaks you: the forgiving streak is the whole point. You can also get 90% of the benefit for free by manually applying "never miss twice" in Streaks or any tracker.

Frequently asked questions

Is Patterns or Streaks cheaper?

Streaks is far cheaper: a one-time $5.99 purchase versus Patterns' subscription of $49.99/year or $9.99/month. Over multiple years the difference is substantial. Patterns' cost buys a forgiving-streak design and if-then habit planning, not just a tracker.

Does Streaks break your streak if you miss one day?

Yes. Streaks uses a classic streak that resets after a single missed day. Patterns is designed so the streak only breaks after two consecutive missed days, implementing the 'never miss twice' rule. You can replicate that discipline manually in Streaks.

Do Patterns and Streaks work on Android?

No. Both are Apple-only. Streaks runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Vision Pro; Patterns runs on iPhone and iPad. Android users should look at Loop (free) or Habitify instead.
