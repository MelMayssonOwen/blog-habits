Streaks vs Habitify: which habit tracker should you pick?

Choose Streaks if you're all-Apple and want a one-time $5.99 purchase with no subscription and a great Apple Watch app. Choose Habitify if you use Android or a Mac alongside your phone, want a free tier for three habits, or need integrations, its Premium is $2.49/month billed annually. Streaks wins on pay-once simplicity within Apple; Habitify wins on cross-platform reach and a free entry point.

Where Streaks wins

No subscription, ever. Pay $5.99 once (verified on the App Store) and it's yours, with a polished Apple Watch experience and up to 24 habits. If you're committed to Apple and value owning software outright, Streaks is hard to beat.

Where Habitify wins

Reach and a free start. Habitify runs on iOS, Android and macOS, has a free three-habit tier, and integrates with Google Calendar and Zapier, none of which Streaks offers. If you ever use a non-Apple device or want automation, Habitify is the safer long-term bet.

Bottom line

All-in on Apple and allergic to subscriptions? Streaks. Multi-platform, or want to try before paying? Habitify. Neither has a meaningfully "smarter" streak than the other, for that, see the forgiving-streak comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Is Streaks or Habitify better for iPhone?

Both are strong on iPhone. Streaks is a one-time $5.99 purchase with an excellent Apple Watch app; Habitify has a free tier and adds Android/Mac sync and integrations. If you never leave Apple and hate subscriptions, Streaks; if you want cross-platform or a free start, Habitify.

Does Streaks sync to Android?

No. Streaks is Apple-only (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Vision Pro). Habitify is the cross-platform choice, running on iOS, Android and macOS.

Which is cheaper long-term, Streaks or Habitify?

Streaks, if you stay on Apple: $5.99 once versus Habitify's ongoing $2.49/month (billed annually) for Premium. But Habitify's free tier covers three habits at no cost, and its $59.99 lifetime option caps the spend.
