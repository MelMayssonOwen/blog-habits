Patterns vs Habitify: which habit tracker should you pick?

Choose Habitify if you use more than one platform (iPhone, Android, Mac), want a genuinely free tier for up to three habits, or need integrations with Apple Health, Google Calendar and Zapier, its Premium is just $2.49/month billed annually or a $59.99 lifetime unlock. Choose Patterns if you're all-Apple and your specific problem is quitting after one missed day: its streak breaks only after two consecutive misses and it plans habits as if-then intentions. Habitify is cheaper and cross-platform; Patterns is more opinionated about the psychology of sticking.

Where Habitify wins

Value and reach. The free tier genuinely covers three habits, enough for most people starting out, and its paid plan is one of the cheapest here at $2.49/month billed annually, or $59.99 once forever. Crucially, it runs on iOS, Android and macOS and integrates with Apple Health, Google Calendar and Zapier, so your data flows into the rest of your systems. If you switch phones or want automation, Habitify is the pragmatic pick.

Where Patterns wins

Design philosophy. Patterns isn't trying to be everywhere or do everything; it targets one problem, abandonment after a slip, with a forgiving streak and if-then planning. It's also the more private option: no accounts, no analytics, data stays in your own iCloud. That focus is the appeal, but it comes at a price (a $49.99/year subscription with no free tier) and it's Apple-only.

Bottom line

For most people, Habitify offers more for less: free to start, cheap to upgrade, and it works across devices. Choose Patterns if you're committed to Apple, value privacy, and specifically need the forgiving-streak psychology, that's what you're paying the premium for.

Frequently asked questions

Is Habitify or Patterns cheaper?

Habitify. It has a free tier for three habits, and Premium is $2.49/month billed annually or a $59.99 one-time lifetime unlock. Patterns has no free tier beyond a two-week trial and costs $49.99/year or $9.99/month.

Does Habitify work on Android?

Yes. Habitify runs on iOS, Android and macOS with cross-platform sync, which is its main advantage over Patterns, Patterns is iPhone and iPad only.

What does Patterns do that Habitify doesn't?

Patterns' streak only breaks after two consecutive missed days (Habitify uses a standard streak), it structures habits as if-then implementation intentions, and it's private by design with no accounts or analytics. Habitify counters with a free tier, lower price, cross-platform sync and integrations.
