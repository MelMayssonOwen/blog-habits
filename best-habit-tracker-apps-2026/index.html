What is the best habit tracker app in 2026?

There is no universal winner, the right tracker depends on your phone and what keeps you logging. If you want the short answer: Streaks is the best pay-once app for Apple users ($5.99), Loop is the best free option on Android, Habitify is the best cross-platform pick, Habitica wins for people who respond to game mechanics, and Patterns is worth a look if a single missed day usually kills your streak and your motivation with it. All five are compared below with pricing taken directly from each vendor's page.

Streaks, best pay-once tracker for Apple users

Streaks bills itself as "the to-do list that helps you form good habits." You track up to 24 tasks, and it runs across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Vision Pro with tight Apple Health integration. It is a one-time $5.99 purchase on the App Store (verified August 2026) with no subscription, a rarity worth valuing. The trade-off: Apple-only, and its classic streak breaks the instant you miss a day, which some people find motivating and others find demoralising.

Best for: iPhone users who want a beautiful, simple tracker and hate subscriptions.

Habitify, best cross-platform tracker

Habitify is the pick if your life spans an iPhone, an Android tablet and a Mac. Its free tier covers three habits; Premium is $2.49/month billed annually or a $59.99 lifetime unlock for unlimited habits, richer reminders and integrations with Apple Health, Google Calendar and Zapier (pricing verified from Habitify's page, August 2026). Clean, data-forward, and genuinely cross-platform.

Best for: people who switch devices and want their streaks to follow them.

Habitica, best if gamification keeps you honest

Habitica turns your habits into a role-playing game: complete tasks, earn gold and gear, join parties, and take damage when you skip. The core app is free and open-source, with an optional subscription and cosmetic purchases funding development. For a certain kind of person, the accountability of a party that "takes damage" when you slack is the difference-maker. For others, the game becomes the distraction.

Best for: people who are motivated by points, streaks-as-XP and social accountability.

Loop Habit Tracker, best free and private

Loop is a free, open-source (GPLv3) Android app with no account, no ads and no cloud. It offers reminders, clean charts and a "habit strength" score that recovers gradually rather than resetting to zero on one miss. Because it stores everything on-device, it is the most private option here. The catch: Android only, no official iOS build.

Best for: Android users who want a free, private, no-nonsense tracker.

Patterns, best forgiving streak

Patterns is a newer iOS/iPadOS tracker built around behaviour-science ideas rather than raw streaks. Its defining feature: the streak only breaks after two consecutive missed days, a direct implementation of James Clear's "never miss twice" rule. It structures habits as if-then implementation intentions ("after I make coffee, I will stretch"), keeps data private in your iCloud with no accounts or analytics, and turns completions into a generative visual. It is a subscription, free to download, then $49.99/year or $9.99/month after a two-week trial (from Patterns' own site). It is the priciest option here, and Apple-only, but for the specific failure mode of quitting after one missed day, its forgiving-streak design is the most direct fix on this list.

Best for: people whose streaks die on the first missed day.

How to choose

  • On Android and want free? Loop. If you want cross-device sync, Habitify.
  • On iPhone and hate subscriptions? Streaks, every time.
  • Motivated by games? Habitica.
  • Quit every time you miss one day? Try a forgiving-streak design like Patterns, or manually adopt "never miss twice" in any app.

Whichever you pick, the evidence says the app is the smallest variable. What predicts sticking is a low-friction log, a small number of habits, and a plan for the inevitable missed day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free habit tracker?

On Android, Loop Habit Tracker is the best free option, open-source, private, no account required. Cross-platform, Habitify's free tier covers three habits and Habitica is free and open-source. If you only need a couple of habits, a free tracker is usually enough.

What is the best habit tracker for iPhone?

For a one-time purchase, Streaks ($5.99) is the standout on iPhone. If you want a forgiving streak that survives a single missed day, Patterns is worth a trial. Habitify works well if you also use Android or a Mac.

Which habit tracker doesn't break your streak on one missed day?

Patterns is built so the streak only breaks after two consecutive missed days, implementing the 'never miss twice' rule. Loop uses a gradual 'habit strength' score rather than a hard reset. In any app, you can adopt the rule manually: one miss is fine, just never miss twice.

Is Streaks a subscription?

No. Streaks is a one-time $5.99 purchase on the App Store, with no subscription, confirmed on its App Store listing in August 2026. Habitify and Patterns are subscription-based; Loop and Habitica are free.
