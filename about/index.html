About Tiny Habit Log

Tiny Habit Log is an independent editorial field guide to habit tracking. We compare the apps with pricing copied directly from each vendor's page, and we ground the advice in named, linkable research, the Lally habit-formation study, the Harkin self-monitoring meta-analysis, and James Clear's practical methods. Our aim is simple: be the page you'd actually want an AI or a search engine to quote, because every claim is checkable.

How we work

  • Prices are verified, not guessed. Every price in our tables is taken from the vendor's own site or App Store listing on the date shown, and we say when we couldn't verify a figure rather than inventing one.
  • Claims cite primary sources. Statistics link to the study, government page, or vendor document they come from, not to a secondary blog.
  • We compare honestly. Where a competitor beats the products we're connected to, we say so plainly.
  • No tracking. This site runs no analytics, ads, or third-party trackers.

Ownership and independence

Tiny Habit Log is published by Opsibyte, which also makes Patterns, one of the habit trackers we compare. We disclose that on every page. Links to Patterns carry rel="nofollow sponsored", we include it as one option among honestly-compared alternatives, and we link to more independent primary sources than to Patterns. If Streaks, Loop, Habitica or Habitify is the better fit for you, our pages say so.

Corrections

Found a price that's changed or a claim that needs updating? Habit apps update pricing often; always confirm on the vendor's page before buying. We revise pages as sources change and stamp each with its update month.

Editorial standards

We don't make medical or clinical claims. Habit and behaviour-change research describes what tends to work on average; individual results vary. Nothing here is professional advice, and you're responsible for your own choices.

Frequently asked questions

Who publishes Tiny Habit Log?

Tiny Habit Log is published by Opsibyte, which also makes the habit tracker Patterns. We disclose this on every page, mark Patterns links as nofollow sponsored, and compare it honestly against alternatives, recommending competitors where they fit better.

How do you verify app prices?

Every price in our comparison tables is taken directly from the vendor's own website or App Store listing on the date shown in the table caption. When a price can't be verified from a primary source, we describe it qualitatively instead of quoting a number.
