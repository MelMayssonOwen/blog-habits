What are the best alternatives to Streaks?

The best Streaks alternatives depend on what pushed you away. If you left because Streaks is Apple-only, use Habitify (iOS/Android/Mac) or Loop (free, Android). If a single missed day resetting your streak demoralised you, try Patterns (breaks only after two misses) or Loop's gradual "habit strength" score. If you want gamification, use Habitica. Streaks itself remains an excellent $5.99 one-time app for disciplined Apple users — most people don't need to leave it.

Should you actually leave Streaks?

Often, no. Streaks is a one-time $5.99 purchase (verified on the App Store) with no subscription — a genuinely good deal. If your only complaint is the harsh streak reset, you can adopt "never miss twice" as a personal rule inside Streaks and keep everything else you like. Switch apps only if you need a different platform, a free option, or a design that forgives the first slip for you.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to Streaks?

On Android, Loop Habit Tracker is the best free, open-source alternative. Cross-platform, Habitify's free tier covers three habits, and Habitica is free and open-source with gamification. Streaks itself is paid ($5.99 one-time).

Is there a Streaks app for Android?

No, Streaks is Apple-only. The closest Android alternatives are Habitify (cross-platform, free tier) and Loop Habit Tracker (free, open-source, Android-only).

What's a good alternative to Streaks that forgives a missed day?

Patterns breaks the streak only after two consecutive missed days, and Loop uses a gradual 'habit strength' score instead of a hard reset. Both are gentler than Streaks' classic streak on a single miss.
